What to do if data is missing

  • Stop using the affected device where possible, especially if files were deleted or the storage is behaving unusually.
  • Avoid saving new files, formatting the device or repeatedly trying recovery tools, as this may reduce recovery options.
  • Keep the device safe and share only its type and a brief description when contacting us.

Recovery cannot be guaranteed. Results depend on the cause of data loss, device condition and assessment. For devices with physical damage, unusual sounds or signs of liquid damage, power it off and ask for advice rather than attempting repairs.
