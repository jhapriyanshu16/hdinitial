Where it began

Our Story

HD Initial started in 2016 as a small company with a simple yet powerful vision: to make practical technology solutions accessible to businesses of all sizes. What began with focused hardware and technical support has grown into a trusted IT partner for organisations across Maharashtra.

We partner with organizations to design, implement, and maintain reliable office networks, security systems, and IT infrastructure. Our tagline, “Everything in Technology – Sales | Service | Rent,” reflects our commitment to being a one-stop solution for IT needs.

The company was formally registered in 2020, and new branches opened in 2022 as the team expanded its ability to support customers across the region. Today, HD Initial serves 1000+ clients with four strategic locations and a growing technology service portfolio.
