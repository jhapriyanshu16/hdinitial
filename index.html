Frequently asked questions

Good to know before you enquire

Where are HD Initial’s branches located?

HD Initial has branches in Virar East (Head Office), Andheri West, Boisar West and Kalyan East. See the branch details above or on the contact page for addresses and phone numbers.

Can I see product prices or live stock online?

Some product pages show example configurations and indicative starting prices, not live inventory or guaranteed offers. Please enquire to confirm current models, specifications, prices, stock and product terms.

Is refurbished computer warranty the same for every product?

No. Warranty varies by product and is confirmed individually. Manufacturer warranty applies to eligible new products according to their respective terms.

Are rental equipment and installation available on fixed rates?

Rates, availability, delivery, pickup, installation and technical assistance depend on equipment and the rental agreement. They are confirmed for each enquiry.

How do I request service or technical support?

Use the relevant service page or contact form for CCTV, computer repair, data recovery, Apple support, networking or AMC enquiries. Share your location and requirements so HD Initial can confirm service coverage and next steps.
