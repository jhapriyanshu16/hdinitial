01 Introduction

HD Initial Private Limited (“HD Initial”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) respects the privacy of visitors and customers who access our website, hdinitial.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect personal information when you visit our website, contact us, submit an enquiry or use our services. By using our website, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

02 Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you interact with our website or contact us.

Personal information

  • Full name
  • Phone number and WhatsApp number
  • Email address
  • Company or organisation name
  • Business address, where required
  • Any other information voluntarily provided in an enquiry or service request

Technical information

When you visit our website, our hosting or server infrastructure may automatically collect certain technical information, such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device information
  • Pages visited
  • Date and time of access

03 How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Responding to customer enquiries and requests.
  • Providing quotations and information about our products and services.
  • Processing service requests and equipment rental enquiries.
  • Providing installation, maintenance, repair and technical support.
  • Communicating important updates regarding our services.
  • Improving website functionality and user experience.
  • Maintaining website security and preventing misuse.
  • Meeting applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

We do not intend to collect more personal information than is reasonably necessary for these purposes.

04 WhatsApp and Other Communication Channels

Our website may provide links that allow visitors to contact us through WhatsApp, email or other communication channels. When you contact us through these services, the information you share may be processed by the respective third-party platform under its own privacy policy and terms.

HD Initial uses information shared through these channels to respond to enquiries and provide business-related assistance.

05 Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website or its hosting infrastructure may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, security and performance. Where applicable, these technologies may collect limited technical information about website usage.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

06 Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent visitors’ personal information to third parties.

We may share relevant information with:

  • Service providers involved in website hosting, maintenance and technical operations.
  • Communication platforms used to respond to customer enquiries.
  • Business service providers where sharing is necessary to fulfil a requested service.
  • Government authorities or law enforcement agencies where required by applicable law.

We only share information to the extent reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

07 Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss or misuse.

However, no method of electronic transmission or internet storage is completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted through the internet.

08 Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including responding to enquiries, providing services, maintaining business records and complying with legal obligations.

When information is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to delete or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable legal requirements.
