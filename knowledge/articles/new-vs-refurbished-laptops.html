New vs refurbished laptops: which should you choose?

The right choice depends on what you need the laptop to do, the device-specific condition and the terms you can confirm before buying.

Start with the work

List the applications you use, the kind of files you handle, how often you travel with the device and whether you need a laptop or desktop. This helps frame the performance, portability and connectivity questions to discuss. Do not select only by a product label or a single specification.

What to check on a new laptop

Ask for the current model details, configuration, price and availability. Confirm which manufacturer warranty applies and review the specific product terms. If the computer is for work, check that it supports the peripherals and software your workflow depends on.

What to check on a refurbished laptop

Refurbished products can differ from one another. Ask about the condition, configuration, included accessories and any item-specific warranty before proceeding. Warranty varies by product and should be confirmed individually; do not assume the same terms apply to every refurbished device.

Compare the whole requirement

Consider the expected use, device condition, support arrangements and confirmed product terms together. A lower initial price does not by itself establish which device is best for your situation. If you already own a computer, an upgrade or repair may also be worth discussing.
