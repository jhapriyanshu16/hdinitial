Attendance technology

What is biometric attendance management?

Biometric attendance systems use a person's physical or behavioural characteristics to support identity checks when recording attendance.

Device and workflow work together

An attendance setup may include a capture device, software or another management process, and the procedures an organisation uses to review attendance information. The right arrangement depends on the workplace, number of users, entry points and existing systems.

Questions to consider

  • What attendance process should the system support?
  • Where will staff use the device, and how many people need it?
  • How will attendance records be accessed and managed?
  • What happens when a device or network is unavailable?
  • What data-handling practices and policies apply to your organisation?

Assess privacy and operations

Biometric information can be sensitive. Organisations should assess applicable legal and internal requirements, communicate transparently with staff and apply appropriate access and retention controls. Seek qualified advice on compliance obligations for your circumstances.
