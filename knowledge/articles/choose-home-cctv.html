How to choose the right CCTV system for your home

A useful CCTV discussion begins with the areas you want to monitor and how the system should fit your home.

Identify coverage priorities

Think about entry points, shared spaces, parking or other areas where visibility is important. A rough sketch or description of the property can help explain the coverage goal. Camera count and placement should be considered in context rather than guessed from floor area alone.

Consider lighting and viewing needs

Describe the lighting conditions and whether you need to view the system locally or remotely. Remote mobile viewing can involve camera, network and configuration requirements that should be confirmed for your setup.

Plan installation and ongoing support

Discuss where equipment could be placed, how cables may be routed and what existing infrastructure is present. Ask about maintenance, repairs and future upgrades. Inspection availability and terms are confirmed individually; do not assume a site visit is free.

Ask about privacy

Consider which areas should be monitored and avoid pointing cameras into spaces where people reasonably expect privacy. Be mindful of household and neighbour privacy, and ask about suitable placement before installation.
