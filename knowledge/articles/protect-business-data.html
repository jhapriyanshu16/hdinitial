How to protect important business data

Reliable data protection combines planned backups, appropriate access and a process for restoring information when needed.

Know what matters

Identify the business data and systems that are important to daily operations. Consider where information is stored, who needs access and how disruption would affect the organisation. This helps establish what should be protected and how often copies may need to be updated.

Maintain separate, managed backups

Keep backup copies in a way that reduces the chance one incident affects both the working data and its backups. Protect access to backup systems and document who is responsible for monitoring them. A backup that has never been checked may not be usable when required.

Test restoration

Periodically confirm that selected information can be restored using your documented process. A recovery test can reveal missing files, access problems or unclear responsibilities before an urgent situation.

Review access and updates

Use individual accounts where appropriate, limit access to what people need and keep systems maintained. Train staff to recognise suspicious requests and follow your organisation's incident procedures.

No single checklist replaces a plan suited to your systems and risks. Consider qualified security and compliance advice for your organisation.
