Commercial CCTV guide

CCTV camera requirements for shops and offices

A commercial CCTV plan should reflect how the premises operate, not just how many cameras can be installed.

Map the space and priorities

Identify entry and exit points, customer-facing areas, counters, shared workspaces, storage and other locations important to your operations. The correct arrangement depends on the layout, viewing angles and what the system is intended to help observe.

Account for working conditions

Lighting, reflective surfaces, operating hours and the placement of existing equipment can affect the discussion. Explain these conditions and whether there are network or power constraints. For larger sites, describe the approximate area and key zones instead of guessing an exact camera count.

Consider access, retention and privacy

Discuss who needs to view the system, whether remote mobile access is required and how recordings should be managed under your organisation's policies. Avoid capturing private spaces unnecessarily and establish appropriate access controls.

Plan for support

Ask how installation, mobile viewing setup, maintenance, repair and future system changes may be handled. Inspection, equipment, pricing and installation schedule are scoped and confirmed individually.
