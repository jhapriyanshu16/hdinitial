Business maintenance guide

Why businesses consider annual maintenance contracts

An AMC can give a business a defined way to plan maintenance and support, provided its scope and terms are clear.

What does an AMC define?

An Annual Maintenance Contract is an agreement for services over a specified period. It should identify the systems or equipment covered, the work included, any exclusions, how requests are handled, the agreed visit schedule and applicable commercial terms.

Preventive maintenance and support

Planned checks may help an organisation review the condition of its technology and address maintenance needs. The actual work depends on the covered equipment and agreed scope. A contract should not be assumed to provide every repair, replacement part or response time unless those terms are explicitly included.

Questions to ask before agreeing

  • Which locations, devices and systems are included?
  • What activities are included or excluded?
  • How are support requests raised and prioritised?
  • What visits or ongoing assistance are part of the arrangement?
  • How are changes to the equipment list handled?

Tailor the agreement to your operation

Document your current environment and business needs before requesting a quotation. Pricing and service expectations should be written into an individual agreement rather than assumed from a generic plan.
