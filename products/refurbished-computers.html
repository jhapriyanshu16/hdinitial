Quality checks and item details

Refurbished computers are quality-tested before being offered. Testing details, cosmetic condition and configuration can vary by item, so ask us for the checks completed on the specific device.

  • Current physical condition and configuration

Clear product terms

Warranty varies by product

There is no single warranty period for all refurbished computers. Warranty, if applicable, is confirmed individually for each product before purchase.

Do not assume new-product manufacturer warranty terms apply to refurbished equipment.
